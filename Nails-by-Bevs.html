Studio policies

Deposit

Your slot is secured with a ₱50 deposit, which is non-refundable. This will be deducted from your final bill.

Cancellations

Once you cancel, the deposit is forfeited, so please only book a date you're sure about.

Payment

Settle your remaining balance in cash or via online bank transfer.

Late arrival

Let me know as early as you can if you're running late. Arriving more than 15 minutes late adds a ₱50 late fee, and 30 minutes or more automatically cancels your appointment.

Important reminders

  • This is a homebased studio, so expect a cozy home setup rather than a full salon.
  • A full set can take 2 to 3 hours. Please use the bathroom before we start.
  • Guests can't join your appointment, so come solo and enjoy the quiet.
